Beginning

In the ancient world, Bathala, the great god of the heavens, ruled over all creation. He had two children: Apolaki, the strong god of the sun, and Mayari, the gentle goddess of the moon. While Apolaki was proud and powerful, Mayari was calm, wise, and caring. After Bathala passed away, the two siblings were left to decide who would rule the world.
