Beginning

Long ago, in a time when gods still walked among the skies, the world was ruled by Bathala, the powerful god of the heavens. He created the earth, the sky, and all living things, bringing light and order to the universe. Bathala had two children who were very different from each other. Apolaki, the god of the sun, was strong, brave, and proud, and his light was bright and fierce. Mayari, the goddess of the moon, was gentle, wise, and calm, and her light was soft and comforting. While Apolaki believed strength made him worthy to rule, Mayari believed leadership required fairness and understanding. After Bathala passed away, the responsibility of ruling the world fell upon the two siblings.
