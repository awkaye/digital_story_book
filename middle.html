Middle

After their father’s death, Apolaki declared that he should rule the world alone because he was stronger and brighter. Mayari disagreed and believed that power should be shared to keep balance in the universe. Their argument slowly turned into anger, and soon the skies trembled as the siblings fought for control. The clash between the sun and the moon caused chaos across the heavens and the earth. During the battle, Apolaki accidentally struck Mayari’s eye with his weapon. Mayari cried out in pain and lost her sight in one eye. Seeing what he had done, Apolaki was overcome with guilt and regret.
