End

Realizing that his pride had caused great harm, Apolaki stopped fighting and asked Mayari for forgiveness. Mayari, despite her pain, chose peace instead of revenge. To restore harmony, the siblings agreed to share the sky rather than rule alone. Apolaki would shine during the day as the sun, bringing warmth and life to the world. Mayari would rule the night as the moon, guiding people with her gentle light. Though her light was softer because of her injury, it became a symbol of calm and hope. The world was finally at peace once again.
