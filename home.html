MAYARI

The goddess of the moon

Mayari is a powerful goddess in Philippine mythology, particularly among the Tagalog people, known as the beautiful goddess of the moon, beauty, war, revolution, and the hunt, daughter of the supreme god Bathala, who shares rule of the world with her brother, Apolaki (the sun god) after a fierce battle where she lost one eye, symbolizing her dimmer, yet resilient, light that rules the night. Her story highlights themes of feminine strength, equality, and leadership, representing the balance between light and dark, pain and power, and compromise.

Legend Teaser 🌙

Before the sun ruled the day and the moon guarded the night, two powerful siblings fought for control of the sky. In a world shaped by gods, pride led to conflict, sacrifice brought balance, and a gentle light was born from pain. Discover the legend of Mayari, the moon goddess whose story teaches us that true strength lies in wisdom, peace, and hope.
